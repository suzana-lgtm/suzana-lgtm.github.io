About

PhD student at the MDC in Berlin, trained in biophysics. I have imaged cells on widefield, confocal and two-photon microscopes and analysed the data with FIJI and Cellpose. I have also prepared samples for cryo-soft X-ray microscopy and reconstructed the synchrotron data, and analysed spatial transcriptomics data in R. I work daily with mammalian cell lines, and I have taught students microscopy theory and given them hands-on demonstrations of confocal and widefield microscopes. I am looking for an application scientist position in life-science instrumentation.
